1. Scan coarse foreground probabilities at `0.3`, `0.4`, and `0.5`.
2. Retain every 8-connected component for audit only.
3. Record patient/case, scan, z, component area/bbox, probability, LR,
   GT coverage, GT IoU, purity, and oracle-keep label.
4. Write complete per-slice rows, including slices with zero candidates.
5. Aggregate proposal recall, slice hit rate, candidates/slice, negative FP

or prediction aggregation.  Candidate audit deliberately preserves the normal
three-part support assignment; only `skip_no_organ_slices=False` expands the
evaluated slice set so negative specificity and FP area are measurable.
